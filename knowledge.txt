Project Phoenix is a confidential internal project launched by NexaCorp in early 2026.
The project lead and head architect for Project Phoenix is Rahul Sharma.
The total budget allocated for Project Phoenix is 4.5 million USD.
The target launch deadline for the beta version is December 15, 2026.
Employees working full-time on Project Phoenix receive an extra monthly bonus of 500 USD and 10 days of additional remote work.
The primary programming language chosen for the Project Phoenix backend is Python using FastAPI.
